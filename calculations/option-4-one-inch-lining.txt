OPTION 4: ONE-INCH INTERIOR LINING ALLOWANCE
Design flow 9,900 CFM (three at 3,300). Six-inch roof duct excluded.
One inch added inward on building wall, outer face, ends and ceiling; existing vent/duct openings retained. Baffles have 1in on upper/lower faces and free tips. Existing 80mm total baffle envelope retained, leaving 29.2mm (1.15in) structural allowance; actual construction remains to be selected. No extra lining is added on top of the former lining-inclusive 80mm thickness.
Finished clear width 92.88in; depth 17.71in.
Finished half-depth projection and tip gap 8.86in; vertical gaps matched to 8.86in.
Lowest finished underside remains 12in above ground. Top/middle/bottom center elevations: 37.59, 25.58, 13.57in.
Nominal controlling full-width area 5.713ft2, versus 6.495ft2 previously: 12.0% reduction.
Mean velocity at 9,900 CFM 1733fpm, versus 1524fpm: 13.7% increase.
Velocity-squared factor 1.292: about 29% higher dynamic pressure at equal flow. This is NOT a calculated total system pressure loss or predicted flow reduction. Loss coefficients, lining surface, turns, hood inlets, ground/tree discharge and fan curve are unverified.
To retain the former nominal passage area at the new finished width would require 10.07in clear gaps. With symmetric half-depth baffles and one inch lining on both walls, raw internal depth would need 22.14in, an increase of 2.42in over current geometry. That conflicts with the tree-driven depth constraint unless the enclosure location/width changes.
FRK identifies a facing, not a verified internal acoustic liner. Exact product, airstream-facing orientation, temperature and moisture suitability require confirmation. No absorption rating or acoustic improvement assigned.
