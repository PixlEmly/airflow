# Design brief and current dimensions

## Purpose and user decisions

Reduce sound emitted by side-wall exhaust vents using a lined enclosure extending approximately to the soffit edge, with an open bottom and a winding path through internal baffles. Use scan-derived references for tree, wall, soffit and vents. Favor simple rectangular construction and a straight tree-side end rather than complex curved cutouts. The selected enclosure must remain reviewable alongside alternatives in one model.

Nominal small duct: 6in. Two nominal larger ducts: 10in each. One 10in opening is under the additional left hood; the other is left of the large square hood. The large square hood is a third main-enclosure outlet. User confirmed approximately 3,300 CFM each for these THREE main outlets, conservatively above actual flow. The separate 6in silencer route is excluded.

## Coordinates and units

Model uses metres. X runs along the wall, Y outward from the building, Z upward from approximate ground. Wall is Y=0. Exterior views look from +Y. Positive X is left when looking squarely at the wall. Scan originals retain their own coordinates; use stored registration and modeled references, not direct overlay without transforms.

Half-depth finished tips meet at one depth coordinate with no overlap; attenuation is not established. Core attachment/support details are not designed.

Preserve finished air passage dimensions and place wall framing outward where measured tree clearance allows. ## Shared vent and roof assembly

Open 10in nominal center: X=+0.204m, Z=1.759m. Separate 6in center: X=-0.181m, Z=1.769m. Left hood 10in center: X=+0.65m, Z=1.759m. Large hood envelope roughly X=-1.39..-0.75m, Y=0..0.38m, Z=1.20..1.84m; its outlet marker bottom is 1.184m. These are reference approximations, not fabrication coordinates.

The six-inch assembly has a short-radius 90-degree lower elbow, nominal 6in centerline radius, into a vertical LD6-type silencer. Reference dimensions interpreted as 6in connectors, 10.25in body diameter, 23.625in body length. Verify whether the dimension-sheet length includes transitions before procurement. Silencer lowered into roof structure. A square 16in chase encloses the full larger-diameter body; only reduced neck and inward-facing short-radius 90-degree top elbow emerge. The earlier 120-degree idea was superseded. Weather shedding, drainage, hot-exhaust compatibility, supports and fire/thermal clearances are not detailed.

Roof plane spans modeled soffit width (about 3.25m) and extends 10ft inward. It is anchored at soffit-front top Z~2.26m. User requested average of corrugated and white scanned patches instead of assumed 2:12 pitch. Fit gives about -0.23:12, back edge roughly 2.29in lower over 10ft. Short scan patch and ~20mm scatter make magnitude/direction uncertain. Ignore flawed/giant scan planes; do not treat this illustration as a surveyed drainage design.

## Tree and other options
