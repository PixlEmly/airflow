# Review plan and open decisions

1. Verify physical dimensions: vent diameters and locations, actual hood discharge geometry, soffit projection, ground elevation and required tree clearance including movement/growth. Check whether all three outlets ever run at full flow simultaneously.
2. Identify each fan and operating speed; obtain curves and permitted exhaust resistance if possible. Measure baseline per-outlet flow and pressure. Do not assume equal flow just because the design allowance is equal.
3. Identify exact one-inch FRK product, air-facing surface, absorption data and service suitability. Confirm proposed baffle structural core and attachment method; the current1.15in core is only an allowance.
4. Set acoustic success criteria: receiver location(s), relevant operating conditions, acceptable level and low-frequency/tonal concerns. Measure baseline spectrum and level with repeatable conditions.
5. Build a reversible full-scale adjustable mockup if practical; measure flows, static pressure and noise before/after, fans individually and together. Common enclosure may cause interaction or flow through inactive outlets; assess it rather than assuming independent performance.
6. If performance fails, study passage area and turning geometry first. Compare number, spacing, projection, edge radius and lining; preserve sufficient acoustic path. Options1–3 need a different internal scheme because raising the base removes available height. Do not simply reuse option4 baffle coordinates.
7. Confirm separate roof duct hardware, actual silencer length convention, roof pitch, rain exclusion, drainage and temperature/support details. The current roof is illustrative and the inward90 elbow is user intent, not validated weatherproofing.
8. Refresh model, portable export, drawings, calculations and handoff after decisions. Do not issue for fabrication until unresolved airflow, sound and physical details have been addressed.

No next geometry variant or construction release has been approved. Current authorization is to preserve and package the review state.
